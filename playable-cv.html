DELIVERY RUN: RECONNECT THE GRID

The grid is down and five homes have lost their connection to the game servers. Deliver a fresh connection to every house before the clock runs out.

🔌Reconnect 5 houses. Touch each house's red NO SIGNAL box. A green line shows it's back online.
⚡Carry 2 packets. Each house uses one. Refill at relay pylons, which are also where you respawn.
🛡Safe zones. Lag spikes can't hurt you inside a relay's glowing dome. Catch your breath there.
⇪Boost pads. Step on a green chevron pad to launch over lag spikes and firewalls.
◈Phase orbs. Grab one to phase straight through lag spikes and firewalls for 4 seconds.
⚠Hazards. Firewalls flicker before switching on. Landing in a sunken data void zaps you — jump straight back out. Every hit costs 5s.
